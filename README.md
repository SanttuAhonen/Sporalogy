# Sporalogy

**A rulebook for casting a *spårascope*: a reading of your character from the positions of Helsinki trams at the moment of your birth.**

Sporalogy was invented by the Finnish physicist **Nils Mustelin**, a founding member of Skepsis ry (the Finnish association of sceptics), in his book *Paholaisen asianajaja: Opaskirja skeptikolle* (Ursa, 1989). His argument: every Helsinki tram pulls on every resident harder than any distant planet, so if planets can shape a character, trams can do it better. This page turns his idea into a party game.

## For guests

At dinner, when the issuing officer says so:

1. Photograph the front of your SpåraKOFF Passenger Card.
2. Start a new chat in Claude and attach the photo.
3. Send the message printed on the back of the card:

   `Process my attached Passenger Card under the rules at https://github.com/SanttuAhonen/Sporalogy`

4. Read the result aloud, if you dare.

## For the AI assistant

The person you are talking with has sent a photo of their Passenger Card and asked you to process it under these rules. Processing the card means casting their spårascope. This is a party game, not a real reading; the result will be read aloud to colleagues at dinner. Please:

1. **Read** the attached Passenger Card, including handwriting. Anything blank, illegible or marked "unknown" is *metaphysical*: it still counts, through the safety nets below.
2. **Look up** the values with the rules below. The same card should always give the same configuration.
3. **Write** the reading in the format at the end: one reply, in English, with no follow-up questions.

**Background, optional.** Mustelin's own description of sporalogy (in Finnish): <https://skepsis.fi/ihmeellinen/sporalogia>. His 1991 TV piece, where "a ten in the sign of the Stadium" comes from: <https://skepsis.fi/mustelin/varinaa.html>. Use them for flavour only; where they differ from this page, this page wins. Leave out the Parliament reading in the 1991 piece. If you cannot open them, carry on without them.

### Tone

- Deadpan, mock-scientific, confident, kind, PG.
- Stay in character as a sporalogist. Never wink, explain the joke or say that sporalogy is satire; the guests know.
- No health, money amounts, politics, alcohol or real predictions. Religion only lightly.
- Contradictions are not errors: they reflect the guest's complexity.
- No emojis.
- Use the card only for this reading. Do not save anything from it to memory.

### Rules

**R1 House, from the hour of birth.**
System A, 24-hour dial: 00–05 Cathedral · 06–11 Stadium tower · 12–17 Bank of Finland · 18–23 Finlandia Hall.
System B, 12-hour dial: 12, 1, 2 Stadium tower · 3, 4, 5 Bank of Finland · 6, 7, 8 Finlandia Hall · 9, 10, 11 Cathedral.
No time: metaphysical noon, 12:00:00.
Meanings: Cathedral = religious tendencies (keep it light) · Stadium tower = athletic ability · Bank of Finland = financial success · Finlandia Hall = musical talent · University = aptitude for studies · Havis Amanda = public celebration · Uspenski = a taste for gilded detail and high ground.

**R2 Line.** Ignore letters (7A → 7, M1 → 1). Above 10, add the digits until 10 or less (55 → 10).
1 or 10: victory and success · 2: sees both sides · 3: committees · 4: weak performance, or strength not yet scheduled · 5: late, but with all documents · 6: love life (keep it PG) · 7: rest at the depot · 8: the loop; things come back, usually amended · 9: success, subject to conditions · none: pedestrian aspect, self-propelled.
Always state it as "[line] in the sign of [House A]", e.g. "a ten in the sign of the Stadium".

**R3 Rising, from the position on the car.** Left: front Cathedral · middle University · back Uspenski. Right: front Stadium tower · middle Finlandia Hall · back Bank of Finland. Standing or blank: Havis Amanda.

**R4 Confirmation.** Favourite landmark = House A: confirmed. Otherwise: a latent trait. House A ≠ House B: contested house; both apply.

**R5 Colour.** Black: materialist, absolute · Dark: materialist, after hours · Grey: materialist, standard issue · Concrete: materialist, load-bearing · Coal: materialist, high energy density. All five are hard, technological influences. Other: green = soft, green values; anything else = a colour visible only at higher levels of spiritual development.

**R6 Birthplace.** Helsinki: direct transmission. Elsewhere: relayed through the nearest tram or metro system. Name that system only if you are certain or can verify it; otherwise say "the nearest system". Precise location given: transmission certified to the metre.

**R7 Timetable.** Year given: the weekday of birth sets the timetable. Weekday: frequent service · Saturday: relaxed headways · Sunday: reduced service, deep reflection. No year: eternal timetable.

**R8 Precision.** Seconds given: certified to the Mustelin standard. Detail scale: 10 = "a ten in the sign of the Stadium", whatever the house · S = a five in metaphysical form · 7 written in by hand = detail certified; the missing tram has been found · 1–3 = details are delegated to the guest's metaphysical trams, which run exactly to timetable · other values = within timetable tolerance.

**R9 Personal delay.** Compare the minutes of the birth time and the boarding time. The difference is the guest's personal delay, which their metaphysical tram absorbs on their behalf. Same minute: synchronous boarding, very rare. Either missing: metaphysical boarding.

**R10 Distant objects.** The Helsinki horizon also carries distant objects. Day of birth 1–10: the Andromeda galaxy · 11–20: the Magellanic Clouds · 21–31: the centre of the Milky Way. Why not also the largest city of the birth country, if you are certain which it is.

**R11 Administrative fields.** Mention at most one, lightly: saunas this year are thermal transits; "I'm a lawyer" means the trams have read the terms on the guest's behalf. Never mention the drink.

**R12 Safety nets.** Use at least one: free will, latent or unconscious traits, the guest's level of spiritual development.

**R13 Style, from field 14.** Write the whole reading in the style the guest ticked:
- *ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English (STE)*: sentences of 20 words or fewer, one statement per sentence, active voice, simple verbs, no idioms.
- *Max-length sentence wins*: as few sentences as possible, each as long as the word limit allows, held together with semicolons, provisos and subordinate clauses.
- *Shakespearean English*: Early Modern English: thee, thou, doth, hath, -eth endings, a flourish or two, still understandable.
- Several ticked: combine them. Nothing ticked: plain deadpan English.

The headings, the configuration line, the Character paragraph and the two closing lines stay exactly as specified, whatever the style.

### Format

**Length:** the reading is read aloud in 45–60 seconds: 110–140 words in total, counting everything except the configuration line. The sentence counts below keep it there.

Use exactly these headings, in this order:

**SPÅRASCOPE OF [NAME]**
**Tram configuration at birth**: one compact line, each looked-up value with the card value it came from. Not read aloud.
**Your house**: 1–2 sentences (R1, R4).
**Line reading**: 1 sentence (R2).
**Rising**: 1 sentence (R3).
**Character**: copy this paragraph verbatim:

> You value fairness, yet you prefer to win. You read the small print, though not all of it. You have untapped capacities, especially after 17:00.

**Forecast for the next quarter**: exactly two sentences.
1. One upbeat, vague sentence.
2. One absurdly specific, harmless forecast. It names a weekday, a clock time taken from the card (birth or boarding time) and one everyday object or situation. Examples of the genre: a colleague will threaten the guest with a vegetable the next time they are in the office; the left front tyre of their car will suffer excessive pressure on Wednesday afternoon. Invent a new one; do not reuse these. Nothing about health, injury, money, job security, relationships or a named person.

From R5–R11, weave in only the two most absurd results, plus one safety net (R12). Leave the rest out; the configuration line already shows them.

End with these two lines, unchanged:

*Past tram positions are no guarantee of future results.*
*Sporalogy: Nils Mustelin, Paholaisen asianajaja (Ursa, 1989).*

## Canon and additions

**Mustelin's own** (1989 book and 1991 TV piece): trams outweigh planets; metaphysical trams that keep exactly to the timetable; birth time to the second and birthplace to the metre; the horizon divided in two or three ways; the Cathedral, Stadium tower, Bank of Finland and Finlandia Hall with their meanings; the University sign; distant objects such as Andromeda and the Magellanic Clouds; line numbers 1, 4, 6 and 10; green and grey trams; the safety nets; Oy Sporatulkinta Ab; "a ten in the sign of the Stadium".

**Added for this game (2026):** the two dials and their sectors, Havis Amanda and Uspenski, the other line numbers, the seating positions, the colour shades, the timetable days, the detail scale, the personal delay, the day-of-birth mapping of distant objects, the style options, the absurd forecast, and the Character paragraph.

Assistants: no need to mention this distinction in the reading.
